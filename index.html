How much does a clean cost?

Cleans start from R450. The final price depends on the size of the rooms you'd like cleaned. WhatsApp us on 072 975 2801 with a few details and we'll send you a clear quote before booking.

How do I book?

Send us a WhatsApp on 072 975 2801, or email Cleanlivingcpt8001@gmail.com. We'll confirm your quote, then arrange a date and time that suits you.

Do I need to provide cleaning supplies?

We'll confirm who provides the household cleaning products and tools when you book, so there are no surprises on the day.

What's included in a standard clean?

Everyday residential cleaning: dusting, wiping accessible surfaces, kitchens, bathrooms, bedrooms and living areas. We'll agree on the exact scope with you before your visit.

Do you offer deep or specialist cleaning?

Not at the moment. We focus on everyday home cleaning and don't currently offer carpet or upholstery cleaning, pressure washing, post-construction or specialist deep cleaning.

Which areas do you cover?

We serve Cape Town and nearby suburbs. If you're unsure whether we reach you, just ask.
